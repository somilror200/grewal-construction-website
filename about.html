About Our Company

Established in 2021

Grewal Concrete and Carpentry was built on the foundation of hard work and precision. Operating out of Beveridge, we serve the greater Melbourne area and the northern growth corridor, providing top-tier concrete, formwork, and framing services to both private clients and major builders.


Meet Harry Grewal

The business is owned and operated by Harry Grewal. With 8 years of hands-on experience in concreting and full certification as a carpenter, Harry brings a unique dual-trade perspective to every job site. Knowing exactly how a frame interacts with a slab means fewer headaches for builders and a perfectly level finish every time.
