Our Services

What We Do

Concrete Slabs

Durable, precision-poured concrete slabs for residential homes, garages, and commercial builds.

Sand Slabs

Specialised sand slab preparation for highly reactive soils common in Melbourne's northern suburbs.

Frames

Complete timber framing services by certified carpenters, built true and straight.

Formwork

Robust formwork set up to handle complex designs and ensure clean, sharp concrete edges.

Driveways

Exposed aggregate, plain, or coloured concrete driveways built to handle heavy vehicles and look great.

Excavation

Site cuts, trenching, and soil removal using our own fleet of modern excavators and trucks.

Piers

Deep bored concrete piers to provide structural stability and anchor slabs on difficult terrain.

Base / Pre-slab

Thorough ground preparation, levelling, and moisture barrier installation before the pour.
